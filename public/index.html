Yahoo-style free chat rooms and messenger in the browser

Y-Chat is an online chat you open like a web page. There is no messenger app to install. Pick a nickname, walk into a public chat room, or open a private window with one person. Guests are welcome. An account is optional, and it does not keep a copy of the conversation.

If you searched for a chat, a chat room, or a simple messenger, this is that page. The rooms are shared with whoever is inside. A private message is not. The words stay on screen while you are talking, and they are not stored as a history.

How to start a chat

  1. Read the terms and the privacy note, then accept them once in this browser.
  2. Come in as a guest with a nickname, or sign in if you already made an account on this computer.
  3. Open a chat room and say hello, or tap a person and send a private instant message.

A nickname starts with a letter. After that it can use letters, numbers, and underscores, and it is 2 to 16 characters long. Two people cannot wear the same name at the same time. LobbyBot is reserved for the greeter in the Welcome Lobby, so that name is not available.

You need JavaScript for the live rooms and the messenger. The page itself, including this guide, is ordinary text a search engine can read before any chat starts.

Public chat rooms

A chat room is a shared conversation. Everyone currently in the room can read what is said there. Rooms are grouped so the list is easy to scan: Friends, Music, Sports, Tech, and Random. Pick a category, or look at all of them. You can also start a room of your own when you want a topic that is not already on the list.

The Welcome Lobby is the front door. LobbyBot says hello the first time you arrive, and it can answer a short line such as hi, help, or ping. It will also reply if you message it in private. The other rooms wait for people. Their topics are the invitation:

On a phone, Rooms, Chat, and People are three screens. The room list scrolls above the tab bar. Opening a room switches you into the conversation, with the composer sitting above that bar. On a wider window the categories, the room, and the people list sit side by side.

Someone who joins after you does not receive the lines from before they arrived. There is no transcript to catch up on. That is deliberate: a room is a live chat, not a record. Tapping Rooms while you are inside a room leaves it and returns you to the list.

A private messenger

The messenger is how you talk to one person instead of the whole room. Open their name from the people list, from the room, or from your buddies. A private window opens. Type, then send. The room does not see that message. The other person sees it if they are online and they have not ignored you.

A phone shows one private chat at a time, as a sheet above the tab bar. A wide screen can keep up to three private windows open along the bottom. You can minimize a window, close it, or ignore someone. Ignore stops their room lines and their private messages from reaching you. You can undo that later.

People who are online show a status: available, away, or busy, plus a short status line if they wrote one. A little sound can play when a private message arrives. You can turn the sound off. Small picture marks in the composer become emoji, the way old chat shorthand used to.

Guest chat, or an account

A guest chat is the fast way in. There is no password. This browser remembers the nickname so the box can be filled in next time. It does not remember the conversation. When the guest leaves, that visit is over and the name is free again.

An account is only for people who want a buddy list and a status kept on the computer that runs Y-Chat. The password is stored as a hash, not as the word you typed. Signing in somewhere else with the same account takes the place of the first visit, so the name is only online once. Guests do not get a buddy list. They can still message anyone who is online.

If the name you want already belongs to an account, the guest door will say so. Sign in, or pick another nickname. If someone is already using the name right now, it is taken until they leave.

What a refresh does to your chat

Room messages and private messages are not saved on the server, and they are not kept as a log in the browser. Refresh the page and the words are gone. You come back as the same guest or account, in the same room, with the same private windows open and empty, so you can continue the conversation without the old text sitting there.

A short drop in the connection is different from a refresh. The page stays open, a small note says it is reconnecting, and the messages you can already see stay on screen. Sign out, or close the tab, and that return does not happen. The next visit starts from the welcome page.

Questions people ask about this chat

Where can I join a free chat room?

Y-Chat has free public chat rooms in the browser. Come in as a guest, pick a room, and talk. No download.

Is there a private messenger?

Yes. Open someone from the people list and send a private instant message. That messenger window is between the two of you while you are both here.

Can I chat online without an account?

Yes. A guest nickname is enough for the chat rooms and the private messenger. An account is optional and only saves buddies on this computer.

Do I have to download a chat app?

No. This is online chat in the browser. Open the page, pick a name, and join a room or message one person.

Can other people read my private messages?

A private message goes to the person you chose, while you are both connected. It is not posted in the room. It is not saved after you refresh.

What happens to the chat when I refresh?

Refreshing clears the messages and brings you back to the same room and the same private chats. The windows return empty. A short disconnect keeps the text on screen in that tab.

Can I start my own chat room?

Yes. From the room list, start a room, give it a name, pick Friends, Music, Sports, Tech, or Random, and add a short topic. You join it as soon as it exists. Custom rooms stay until the server restarts; the built-in rooms return when it comes back up.

Who is LobbyBot?

LobbyBot is the greeter in the Welcome Lobby. It says hello when you arrive, and it answers a few simple lines such as hi, help, or ping. You can also send it a private message.

Is this Yahoo Messenger?

No. Y-Chat is an independent chat with public rooms and private messages. It is not affiliated with Yahoo.

Is there a Yahoo-style chat room online?

Y-Chat offers free public chat rooms and a private messenger with a classic Yahoo Messenger-style layout in the browser. Join as a guest with a nickname—no download required.

Does a guest stay online after I close the page?

No. A guest is here only while the page is open. Closing the tab frees the nickname. An account can sign in again, and it still only keeps buddies and a status, not the chat.
