Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how Y-Chat handles information when you use our browser-based chat rooms and messenger. Y-Chat is an independent project and is not affiliated with Yahoo. It works together with our Terms & Conditions, including the 18+ eligibility and open chat policy.

Who this applies to

Y-Chat is intended for users who are at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where they live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Open chat and what others can see

Public room messages are visible to everyone in that room while they are connected. Private messages are delivered to the person you choose while you are both online. Other users can see your nickname, status, and what you type in shared spaces. Treat open chat like a public conversation, not a private diary.

What the relay stores

The WebSocket relay keeps live session state (who is in which room, nicknames online, and messages in flight). It does not store a long-term chat history or transcript of room or private messages. When the relay restarts, custom rooms and live presence reset; built-in rooms return when the service comes back.

Operators of a deployment may log technical data (such as connection errors or abuse mitigation) depending on how they host the relay. The public deployment is designed to minimize server-side retention of message content.

What stays in your browser

  • Legal acceptance: A flag that you accepted the current Terms and this policy (version number), so we do not ask every visit.
  • Guest nickname: Your last guest name may be saved to pre-fill the sign-in box.
  • Optional account: If you register, username, password hash, buddy list, and status are stored locally on your device (not on the relay).
  • Session restore: While the tab is open, the app may remember your room and open private windows so a refresh can return you to the same place (messages themselves are cleared on refresh).

Clearing site data or using a different browser removes this local information.

Passwords

Optional account passwords are hashed before storage in your browser. We do not store your password in plain text on the relay server because accounts are local to your device.

Advertising

Advertising is off unless the operator enables it. If Google AdSense is used, ads are labeled “Advertisement,” appear in designated slots (not inside the live message stream), and may use cookies or similar technologies under Google’s policies. Ads do not load until you accept the Terms and this policy.

Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you accept, and only when a measurement ID is configured. It may receive page URLs and standard browser or device information. It does not receive nicknames, room names, or message content from Y-Chat.

Your choices

You can refuse to accept and not use the service. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser to reset legal acceptance and local account data. You can use guest mode without creating an account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. If we need a new acceptance, the app will ask again before you can chat.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of the Y-Chat site you are using.
